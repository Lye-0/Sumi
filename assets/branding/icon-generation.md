# Sumi icon v1

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Master image: sumi-icon-v1.png.
Concept: crop corners and one ink dot; ink-charcoal and pale mint.
This is the generated artwork master. scripts/Generate-Icon.ps1 packages nine sizes into src/Sumi/Assets/Sumi.ico, embedded in the executable and used by windows and the tray.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square app icon for Sumi, a quiet Windows app that captures selected screen regions and receives answers from a local language model. Brand concept: Japanese sumi (ink) and sumi (corner). Single icon only, no presentation board. A deep ink-charcoal rounded square tile #192129, viewed perfectly straight on, with a very subtle smooth glass-like tonal depth confined to the background. Central symbol: exactly TWO opposing substantial rounded L-shaped crop corners, upper left and lower right, in pale mint #B2E3D6. They suggest selecting a rectangle and are open at the other two corners. Between them, one modest solid mint ink dot, almost circular with a very subtle organic asymmetry, symbolizing a distilled answer. Large clean geometric silhouettes, generous negative space, exceptionally restrained and distinctive, legible at small Windows icon sizes. Corner symbol occupies about 60 percent of tile. Thick uniform strokes with softly rounded caps, central dot distinctly separated from strokes. Tile fills 92 percent of image with genuinely transparent outside rounded corners, no outer cast shadow. No lettering, no S, no sparkles, no brains, no camera, no speech bubble, no extra dots, no mockup, no perspective, no fine details. High-resolution production icon, crisp edges.
